About

My name is Subas Ali. I am an Assistant Lecturer and a PhD Candidate jointly affiliated with the University of Pécs (Hungary) and La Sapienza University of Rome (Italy), as part of the Joint Doctoral Program in Social Representations, Culture and Communication.

I teach and supervise undergraduate courses in social psychology, while my doctoral research examines decolonial representations and positionalities within wider academic structures.
